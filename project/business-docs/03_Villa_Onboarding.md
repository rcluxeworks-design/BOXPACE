# Villa onboarding

## Target profile discussed

Independent villas around Karjat, Khopoli, Lonavala and nearby areas; primarily 3–5 BHK (with 3–6 BHK also suggested for prospecting). Groups and family stays are the intended fit.

## Suggested onboarding sequence

1. Identify the owner or authorised decision-maker using public business details or a permission-based introduction.
2. Record property location, capacity, bedroom/bathroom count, amenities, access, house rules and operational contact.
3. Visit or otherwise verify condition, safety, cleanliness, photographs and guest-ready facilities.
4. Confirm ownership/authority, payout details, taxes/permissions, insurance and any restrictions with the owner; retain appropriate evidence securely.
5. Agree service scope, fees, commission basis, cancellation/refund responsibilities and term in writing.
6. Collect approved photos, descriptions, policies, rate floor/ceiling and calendar blocks.
7. Create or improve OTA listings with owner approval; confirm account ownership and access controls.
8. Test calendars, enquiry response, booking confirmation, payment and owner settlement workflow before going live.

This is a proposed checklist, not a statement that any villa has been onboarded. No villa names or owner details were present in the available material.
