# Lead management

## Two distinct pipelines

1. **Owner acquisition:** villa prospects → decision-maker verified → contacted → interested → meeting → proposal → negotiation → signed / lost.
2. **Guest reservations:** enquiry → dates and guest count qualified → villa options / quote → follow-up → availability/payment confirmed → booking / lost.

## Suggested starting tools

Google Sheets plus WhatsApp Business labels were recommended for an initial five-villa scale. A CRM such as HubSpot was mentioned as a later option if volume and team needs grow. No system has been implemented in the evidence available.

## Guest lead fields

Lead ID, received date, name, preferred contact method, contact permission, dates, guest count, preferred area, budget, event/occasion if volunteered, source, suitable property, quote, next follow-up, status, outcome and booking value.

## Owner lead fields

Property/locality, public business contact or source of permitted introduction, bedroom count, OTA presence, decision-maker verification, first-contact date, interest, next action/date, proposal status and outcome.

## Weekly measures

Enquiries, qualified leads, quotes, follow-ups completed, bookings, conversion rate, marketing cost per confirmed booking, owner meetings, proposals and signed properties. Example rates in the conversation were illustrative only.
