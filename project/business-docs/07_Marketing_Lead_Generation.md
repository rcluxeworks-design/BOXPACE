# Marketing and customer lead generation

## Customer acquisition ideas discussed

- Instagram and Facebook ads using villa video and a WhatsApp/message or lead-form call to action.
- Google Search for high-intent phrases such as private villa in Karjat or group villa in Lonavala.
- Referrals and partnerships with event planners, travel agents, corporate groups and activity providers.
- Direct WhatsApp enquiries and reusable property content.

## Suggested ₹10,000 monthly test split

An earlier suggestion allocated ₹6,000 to Meta WhatsApp ads, ₹2,000 to a Google Search test, ₹1,000 to referral incentives and ₹1,000 to retargeting / the best-performing ad. This was a proposed allocation, not confirmed spend. The accompanying advice was to begin with Meta and add Google after a booking process is ready.

## Audience and offer

Potential guest audience: people in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane planning group or family weekend trips to Karjat, Khopoli or Lonavala. The ₹12,000/night starting price appeared in sample copy and must be checked against live property rates and dates before publishing.

## Measurement

Track source, enquiries, qualified enquiries, dates/guest count received, calls connected (where requested), quotes, bookings, booking value, ad spend and cost per confirmed booking. Contact people who have requested contact or otherwise consented; do not use purchased private contact lists.
