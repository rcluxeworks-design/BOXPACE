# Services

## Services discussed

- Create and maintain property listings.
- Manage availability and calendars.
- Support pricing and revenue optimisation.
- Distribute listings through Airbnb, Booking.com and MakeMyTrip.
- Respond to guest enquiries and coordinate booking communication.
- Generate direct customer enquiries.
- Provide booking reports and coordinate with owners.

## Scope to define per property

Before offering a service, agree who owns photography, listing account access, rate approval, discounts, guest screening, payment collection, check-in, housekeeping, maintenance, emergency response, refunds and damage claims. The conversation does not establish that Boxpace provides physical villa staffing or maintenance.

## Service levels to decide

Set response hours, response-time targets, escalation contacts, reporting cadence, service exclusions and the method for measuring performance. Do not imply guaranteed occupancy or revenue.
