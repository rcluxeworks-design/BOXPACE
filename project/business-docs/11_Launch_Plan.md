# Launch plan

The following sequence is a practical proposal based on the conversation, not a confirmed schedule.

## Phase 1 — Decide the offer

- Confirm business name to use publicly: Boxpace Hostings, Boxpace Hospitality, or both with a consistent relationship.
- Select target area and initial villa profile.
- Choose one owner pricing model and define commission basis, scope and exclusions.
- Confirm service boundaries and owner responsibilities.

## Phase 2 — Make the first properties launch-ready

- Identify and qualify owner prospects; hold owner meetings.
- Verify property authority, readiness, rules and operating responsibilities.
- Sign property-specific agreements.
- Gather accurate photos/content, rates, calendars and policies.

## Phase 3 — Configure systems

- Select OTA account approach and a channel manager only after cost and connectivity checks.
- Set up WhatsApp Business and a lead/booking register in Sheets.
- Test calendar, enquiry, payment, booking, cancellation and settlement workflows.

## Phase 4 — Soft launch and measure

- Begin with the proposed five-villa target only if properties are signed and ready; five is a target, not current inventory.
- Launch a small, trackable marketing test within the discussed ₹10,000/month budget if approved.
- Review enquiries, quote-to-book conversion, guest experience, owner settlement and actual costs weekly.
- Adjust rates and spend from observed results; do not treat illustrative lead costs or starting prices as promises.

## Gate before scaling toward 10 villas

Document repeatable onboarding, owner reporting, guest coverage, calendar accuracy, economics and support capacity before adding inventory.
