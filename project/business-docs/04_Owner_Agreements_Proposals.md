# Owner agreements and proposals

No signed agreement or finished proposal appears in the available conversation. Use this as a drafting checklist and have the final terms reviewed appropriately before signing.

## Proposal should explain

- Boxpace identity and service scope.
- Property-specific observations and proposed improvements.
- Platforms and booking channels to be used.
- Pricing model selected for that owner.
- Responsibilities, reporting and communication process.
- Launch requirements and indicative timeline, with dependencies.

## Agreement terms to settle

- Parties, authority, property address and term/renewal.
- Exclusive or non-exclusive appointment; any owner self-booking rules.
- Exact fee, commission base, treatment of taxes/platform charges, billing and payment dates.
- Who controls listing accounts, guest funds, deposits, refunds and owner payouts.
- Rate approval, availability/calendar responsibility and booking acceptance.
- Guest communication, check-in, cleaning, maintenance and emergency responsibilities.
- Cancellation, damage, complaints, insurance, indemnity and liability allocation.
- Data handling, marketing permissions, termination, handover and dispute process.

Pricing options in the source conflict; do not insert one as a universal agreed rate. See [Pricing and revenue](06_Pricing_Revenue.md).
