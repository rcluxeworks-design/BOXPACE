Service choices

Support can be tailored to your villa.

We can shape the level of support around your property and preferred booking process. We will prepare a property-specific written proposal so you can review the exact scope before committing.

A
Broader booking support

May include OTA listing care, pricing and calendar coordination, enquiries, guest communication, booking coordination, agreed caretaker liaison and reporting.

B
OTA and calendar support

May focus on authorised OTA listings, rates, availability, reservation coordination and booking reports, with the owner handling direct enquiries and on-site operations.

Scope, fees, commission basis, taxes, platform charges and responsibilities must be confirmed in the written agreement. The owner or caretaker remains responsible for physical ground operations unless separately agreed.
