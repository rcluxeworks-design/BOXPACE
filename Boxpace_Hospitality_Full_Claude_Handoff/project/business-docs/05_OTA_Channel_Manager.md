# OTA and channel manager setup

## Platforms named

Airbnb, Booking.com and MakeMyTrip.

## Software direction discussed

An affordable channel manager / property-management system was considered, with a broader software budget of approximately ₹7,000–₹9,000 per month. Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business and Google Sheets were also mentioned. No vendor or subscription is confirmed.

## Setup checklist

- Confirm owner consent, account ownership and user access for each OTA.
- Standardise property facts, occupancy, amenities, house rules, photos and cancellation terms.
- Agree rate plan, taxes/fees display, minimum stays and availability controls.
- Select software only after confirming supported OTA connections, total cost, onboarding, support and contract terms.
- Map room/property inventory and test two-way availability and reservation updates.
- Test modification, cancellation, blocked dates, duplicate prevention and reconciliation.
- Define daily exception monitoring and account recovery/access removal when the relationship ends.

Platform connectivity and product capability must be verified with the chosen vendor before purchase; the conversation contains no vendor research or integration confirmation.
