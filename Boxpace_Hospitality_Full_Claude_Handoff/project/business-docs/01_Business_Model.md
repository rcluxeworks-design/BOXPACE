# Business model

## Core offer

Boxpace partners with villa owners in Karjat, Khopoli, Lonavala and surrounding Maharashtra markets. It helps manage short-stay distribution and the guest booking journey, while owners retain the property and approve commercial terms.

## Customers

- **Supply side:** villa owners / authorised property decision-makers.
- **Demand side:** families, friend groups and other group travellers from Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane and nearby markets (target audience suggested in the conversation).
- **Potential B2B sources:** travel agents, corporate groups, event organisers and local activity providers; these are prospective channels, not established partnerships.

## Potential revenue streams

1. Fixed monthly management fee per property.
2. Commission on bookings, with the commission base and exclusions to be agreed.
3. Direct bookings and B2B group bookings, subject to a clear owner settlement policy.

The fixed fee and commission combinations discussed are alternatives, not cumulative confirmed pricing. See [Pricing and revenue](06_Pricing_Revenue.md).

## Early scale

The conversation proposed onboarding 5 villas initially and aiming for 10. Those are targets, not current inventory counts. Property mix was described as mainly 3–5 BHK; another note used 3–6 BHK as a prospecting filter.

## Operating functions proposed

Sales / owner acquisition; marketing; reservations; revenue management; property operations and owner reporting. Staffing, service hours and whether Boxpace will provide on-site services remain unconfirmed.
