# Operations

## Operating functions proposed

Sales (owners), marketing (guest demand), reservations (enquiry-to-booking), revenue management (pricing/calendars) and operations (guest coordination / owner reporting). These are proposed responsibilities, not confirmed staffed departments.

## Booking workflow to establish

Enquiry → check dates and fit → quote with property-specific terms → owner/availability approval as required → secure reservation and payment → send guest instructions → coordinate arrival/stay → handle issue escalation → check-out and property review → reconcile booking and owner payout → report.

## Controls to define

- One source of truth for availability and reservation status.
- Guest response and escalation coverage.
- Payment, refund, deposit and reconciliation ownership.
- Check-in/out, housekeeping, maintenance and incident roles.
- Owner reporting cadence and booking statement format.
- Guest data access and retention.

No operating team, service-level commitments, live booking procedure or physical property services were confirmed in the available conversation.
